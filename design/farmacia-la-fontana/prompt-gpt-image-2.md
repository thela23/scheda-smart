STYLE: flat two-colour vector, crisp geometric construction, consistent stroke weight, precise curves, balanced negative space, strong silhouette that survives at 16 px and as a backlit shop sign. Swiss-Italian restraint, heritage without nostalgia.

FINISH: clean vector flat on the paper colour, like a brand-guidelines presentation page. Quality: award-level identity design.

CONSTRAINTS: original design; no watercolor, no gradients, no shading, no 3D, no texture, no drop shadows, no glow; no clip-art fountain, no illustration; no extra text besides "FARMACIA" and "La Fontana"; spell both exactly; no watermark; not resembling any existing brand logo.
